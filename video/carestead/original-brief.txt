Create a polished 45 to 60 second product video for Carestead.

First inspect the existing Carestead repository and reuse its logo, colors, typography, branding, and UI patterns. Build the video separately and do not modify the working application.

PRODUCT STORY

Carestead is a caregiver cognitive load agent.

The problem is not that care information does not exist. It already lives across calendars, messages, notes, documents, responsibilities, contacts, and people.

The problem is that someone still has to connect it all:

What changed?
Who does it affect?
What needs attention?
What could fall through the cracks?
What should happen next?

Carestead helps carry that coordination load.

The key distinction should be:

“A calendar knows an appointment changed. Carestead helps understand what that change means for everything around it.”

STYLE

Create a premium, warm, cinematic technology product film.

Use photorealistic people, realistic home environments, natural lighting, subtle movement, and polished mobile UI.

Maya is a capable working woman in her late 30s who coordinates care for Alex while balancing work and family.

Carestead should primarily appear as a mobile app on Maya’s smartphone.

Avoid cartoons, figurines, robots, futuristic AI visuals, hospitals, or stereotypical elderly care imagery.

SCENE 1 — THE LOAD
0:00–0:07

Maya is getting ready for work.

Her phone receives:

“Alex’s physiotherapy moved to 3:30 PM.”

Thoughts appear around her:

“Meeting at 3”
“Who can drive Alex?”
“Does Priya know?”
“What else does this affect?”
“What am I forgetting?”

These represent mental load, not notifications.

Voiceover:

“In caregiving, one small change rarely stays small. An appointment moves, and suddenly there’s a whole new plan to coordinate.”


SCENE 2 — THE PROBLEM
0:07–0:13

Show Maya moving between calendar, messages, notes, tasks, contacts, and care information on her phone.

The information exists, but Maya is connecting everything herself.

On screen:

“Someone still has to connect the dots.”

Voiceover:

“The information may already be there. The hard part is connecting what changed, who it affects, and what needs to happen next.”

0:21–0:30

Maya taps the microphone in the Carestead mobile app and says:

“Carestead, what should I know about Alex today?”

Show Carestead connecting:

Physiotherapy moved to 3:30
→ Maya unavailable
→ Transportation affected
→ Visit preparation has no owner

Then show:

“2 things need attention”

“Transportation coverage needs review”

“Visit preparation has no owner”

Include “View evidence.”

Do not make this look like generic chatbot output.

Voiceover:

“Carestead connects what changed to the people and responsibilities around it, helping surface what could fall through the cracks.”

On screen:

“NOTICE. CONNECT.”
