Original download and license references are recorded in [the earlier commercial's source file](../pitch/SOURCES.md). The depicted people illustrate the story; they are not Carestead customers or endorsers, and the narrator is not their voice. No voice cloning, customer testimonial, or generated human imagery is used.

Narration: Microsoft Edge speech synthesis, `en-US-JennyNeural`, generated from the campaign script with `edge-tts`. Only public campaign copy was submitted to the speech service. No care records or reference documents were sent.

Music and notification sounds: original procedural synthesis in `render_ad.py`; no sampled music.

Product insert: `assets/overview.png`, captured from the running Carestead application's read-only guest sample. Records are fictional. The final edit shows the care-brief panel; it does not stage an approval or claim a task was completed.

Visual pattern reference: the user-supplied `docs/pitch-deck/smartads-video.mov`. Its concise headlines, live footage, visual examples, and pacing informed the edit. None of its footage, soundtrack, voice, logos, or claims are reused.
