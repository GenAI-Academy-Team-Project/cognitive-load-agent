# Footage and audio sources

The final commercial uses two live-action stock clips:

- Pavel Danilyuk, [A Woman Talking on the Phone in the Kitchen](https://www.pexels.com/video/a-woman-talking-on-the-phone-in-the-kitchen-5303249/), Pexels video 5303249. Download: https://videos.pexels.com/video-files/5303249/5303249-hd_1920_1080_30fps.mp4
- Gustavo Fring, [Elderly Couple Drinking Tea](https://www.pexels.com/video/elderly-couple-drinking-tea-6285977/), Pexels video 6285977. Download: https://videos.pexels.com/video-files/6285977/6285977-uhd_3840_2160_30fps.mp4

The [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/) allows free use and modification, including product promotion. It prohibits implying endorsement by depicted people and portraying identifiable people offensively. This edit uses fictional voiceover and labels the conversation as dramatized. Footage is illustrative, not a customer testimonial.

Dialogue is synthesized locally with macOS voices Samantha, Daniel, and Karen; no voice cloning. Background music is synthesized in the renderer, with no sampled music.

A built-in imagegen storyboard was explored before the request was clarified to real footage. It is not used in this commercial. Its prompt described illustrated scenes of a caregiver on the phone, a brother answering, siblings reviewing a tablet, and family tea. The final footage replaces that visual direction.
